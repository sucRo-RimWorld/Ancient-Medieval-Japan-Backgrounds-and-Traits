# Ancient & Medieval Japan - Backgrounds & Traits
# Prepared for first release. Steam upload and visibility are not implied by this file.

0.1.0 - Initial historical backgrounds and traits

Backgrounds
- Rewrite 126 existing backgrounds (123 earlier rewrites and three original Core occupations).
- Add 12 normal-generation backgrounds: adulthood (8): shrine officiant; shrine ritual attendant (shrine miko for women); itinerant ritualist (traveling miko for women); itinerant woodturner; falconer; potter; coastal fisher; coastal salt maker. Childhood (4): hunter's child; traveling artisan's child; fishing village childhood; weaver's child.
- Exclude 135 anachronistic existing backgrounds from selection for newly generated pawns.
- Adjust 23 existing starting-possession records.

Traits
- Add likes alcohol (+2 mood while intoxicated) and dislikes alcohol (−2 mood while intoxicated).
- Exclude Transhumanist, Body Purist and Drug Desire traits, including teetotaler, from normal new-pawn generation.

RimWorld 1.6 / 1.7; Harmony required; Medieval Overhaul optional.
Focused original 1.7 Japanese Vanilla and original 1.6 MO + separated Culture/Characters game tests passed. Old saves, MO 1.7 and individual background natural selection frequencies are not verified.
