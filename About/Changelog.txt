# Ancient & Medieval Japan - Culture Characters
# Prepared for first release. Steam upload and visibility are not implied by this file.

0.1.0 - Initial historical backgrounds and traits
- Rewrite the names and descriptions of 126 existing original backgrounds for ancient-to-medieval Japan (123 existing historical rewrites and three original Core occupations).
- Add 12 new ordinary-generation candidates: eight adulthood and four childhood biographies. Keep two additional duplicate new background IDs for compatibility but exclude them from ordinary generation.
- Restrict normal selection of 135 anachronistic existing backgrounds without deleting their definitions.
- Adjust 23 original starting-possession records.
- Suppress ordinary generation of Transhumanist, BodyPurist and DrugDesire while preserving old definitions; add two alcohol-only preference traits with mood effects while intoxicated.
- Support RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7; Harmony is required. Medieval Overhaul and current split AMJ Culture are optional.
- Focused original 1.7 Japanese Vanilla and original 1.6 MO 1.6.2.2 + separated Culture/Characters game tests passed. Old saves, MO 1.7 and individual natural selection frequencies are not verified.
