# Workshop description draft / Workshop説明文（未公開）

> Draft for first publication. This document is not evidence that a Steam item exists or has been updated. Counts are based on `Docs/Design.md` and shipped BackstoryDefs.

## English

Ancient & Medieval Japan - Culture Characters adapts RimWorld's character backgrounds and traits for ancient-to-medieval Japanese settings.

**Background content (rewrites and additions are different):**
- **126 existing backgrounds rewritten**: 123 previously revised biographies plus 3 original Core occupations. Names and descriptions are adapted to the period; they are *not* newly added backgrounds.
- **12 new playable backgrounds**: 8 adulthood and 4 childhood biographies eligible for ordinary pawn generation.
- 14 new background IDs exist in the files, but 2 overlapping jobs are excluded from normal generation and kept only for compatibility with existing pawn IDs.
- **135 existing backgrounds restricted** from ordinary generation because they are out of period. Definitions are retained, not deleted.
- **2 new traits**: alcohol lover / alcohol averse. Historical starting possessions are also adjusted for 23 original backgrounds.

Requires Harmony. Supports RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7. Medieval Overhaul and current separated AMJ Culture are optional. **Do not combine with older unsplit AMJ Culture versions**, which contain duplicate generation rules. New games are recommended. Scoped native 1.7 Vanilla and 1.6 Medieval Overhaul + separated Culture/Characters tests passed; MO 1.7, old-save loading and all individual random selection rates are not verified.

## 日本語

Ancient & Medieval Japan - Culture Characters（中世日本 - 文化・人物）は、RimWorldの人物背景・特性を古代から中世の日本に合わせて調整するModです。

**既存背景の改稿と新規追加は別の件数です。**
- **既存背景126件を改稿**：従来の123件に加え、Core職業背景3件の名称・説明を中世日本向けに変更。新規背景を126件追加するわけではありません。
- **新規背景12件を追加**：成人8件・幼少期4件。通常のポーン生成対象です。
- 新規背景の定義は計14件ですが、うち重複する職業2件は既存の識別子との互換維持用で、通常生成から除外しています。
- **既存背景135件の通常生成を制限**：時代に合わない背景を通常生成対象から除外します。定義そのものは削除しません。
- **新規特性2件**：酒好き・酒嫌い。既存背景23件の初期所持品も変更します。

Harmonyが必須です。RimWorld 1.6・1.7対応。Medieval Overhaulと分離後の現行AMJ Cultureは任意です。**分離前の旧AMJ Cultureとの同時導入はできません**。新規ゲームを推奨します。1.7 Vanillaおよび1.6 Medieval Overhaul＋分離済みCulture/Charactersでは限定的な実ゲーム検証済みです。MO 1.7・旧セーブ・個別の抽選頻度は未検証です。
